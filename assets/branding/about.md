**rcaiman-projects**
Version 0.1.0

The workbench of the rcaiman suite. 

Organize projects. Preserve provenance. Enable reproducibility.

Scientific processing is performed by *rcaiman*.
Instrument calibration records are managed by *rcaiman.registry* and
distributed through *rcaiman.registry.data*.
